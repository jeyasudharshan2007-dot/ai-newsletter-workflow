Project Limitations

1. AI Accuracy

AI-generated summaries may contain errors. Important facts must be checked against the original sources.

2. Source Reliability

The quality of the newsletter depends on the reliability and freshness of the selected sources.

3. Automation

The workflow is currently documented as a plan. Fully automated collection and publishing have not yet been confirmed as implemented.

4. Human Review

A human editor should review summaries, headlines, links, and the final newsletter before publication.

5. Future Improvements

- Test the workflow with real news articles.
- Record test results.
- Explore automated news collection.
- Improve fact-checking and editorial review.

Conclusion

The project demonstrates a planned AI-assisted newsletter process. Further testing and implementation are needed to evaluate its reliability.
