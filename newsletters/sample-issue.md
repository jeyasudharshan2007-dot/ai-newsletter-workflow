Story 2: Anthropic Reports Progress on AI-Assisted Drug Development

Publisher: Anthropic
Published: October 27, 2026

Anthropic described research partnerships with six organizations working on new medicines. The company reported that its Claude AI system supported tasks including molecular design, research analysis, and documentation.

The work remains at an early stage, and much of the research described is preclinical rather than testing medicines in humans.

Why it matters: AI could assist scientists with parts of the drug-development process, but laboratory testing, clinical trials, and expert review remain essential.

"Read the original announcement" (https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development)

Story 3: AI for Science Program Adds Research Partners

Publisher: Anthropic
Published: October 28, 2026

Anthropic announced an expansion of its AI for Science program involving 20 scientific foundations, institutions, and research groups. The program provides Claude credits to support selected scientific projects.

The announcement describes potential uses including generating research hypotheses, designing experiments, and analyzing complex data.

Why it matters: Access to AI tools may help researchers explore questions and analyze information. The real value depends on research quality, reproducibility, and careful human evaluation.

AI Tip of the Week

Use AI as an assistant, not as your source of truth.

When researching a news story:

1. Find the original article.
2. Ask AI to summarize the article.
3. Compare the summary with the original.
4. Verify dates, names, figures, and important claims.
5. Include the original source link.
