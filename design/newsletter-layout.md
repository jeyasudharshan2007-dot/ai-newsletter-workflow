Newsletter Layout Design

Project

CAPSTONE 51 — AI Newsletter Production Workflow

Newsletter Name

AI & Technology Weekly

Design Objective

Create a clear, professional, mobile-friendly newsletter that makes AI and technology news easy to read.

Target Audience

Students, beginners, and readers interested in artificial intelligence and technology.

Layout Structure

1. Header

- Newsletter name: AI & Technology Weekly
- Issue number and publication date
- Short tagline: Your weekly AI and technology briefing

2. Welcome Section

- A short introduction to the week's main developments
- A brief explanation of the newsletter topic

3. Main News Stories

Each story should contain:

- Headline
- Publisher and publication date
- Short summary
- Why the story matters
- Link to the original source

4. AI Tip of the Week

- One practical AI research or productivity tip
- A short explanation
- A responsible-use reminder when relevant

5. Editor's Note

- A short note about source verification
- Any important limitations or corrections

6. Footer

- Newsletter name
- Issue number
- Publication date
- Source attribution
- Contact or feedback information, if available

Visual Design

- Primary colour: Dark navy blue
- Secondary colour: Light blue
- Background: White
- Body text: Dark grey or black
- Headings: Bold and easy to distinguish
- Font: A clean, readable sans-serif font
- Images: Use only relevant images with appropriate usage rights and attribution when required

Readability Guidelines

- Keep paragraphs short.
- Use clear headings and subheadings.
- Make links easy to identify.
- Avoid overcrowding the page.
- Ensure the layout works on mobile screens.
- Include source links for every news story.

Design Tool

Canva Free can be used to create the visual newsletter layout.

Quality Checklist

- [ ] Newsletter title and issue date are visible.
- [ ] Headlines are clear.
- [ ] Stories include source links.
- [ ] Text is readable on mobile.
- [ ] Images have appropriate usage rights.
- [ ] Spelling and grammar have been checked.
- [ ] Final content has been verified before publication.
