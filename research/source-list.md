Research Rules

1. Prefer original announcements, research papers, and reputable news organizations.
2. Never invent article titles, dates, quotations, or URLs.
3. Use AI to assist with research, not as a replacement for source verification.
4. Record the date research was conducted.
5. Only use verified material in the final newsletter.
